# SEO Page Launch Checklist

- Focus keyword appears in URL, H1, title tag, meta description, first 100 words and one H2
- Master pages target 900+ words
- Geo pages target 800+ words
- Meta title and meta description are unique
- Internal links include parent master, sibling geo pages, cross-service link and sitemap
- Schema markup is validated
- Mobile performance and Core Web Vitals are checked
- Tracking hooks are in place before launch
- Tier 1 geo pages include manual local enrichment
