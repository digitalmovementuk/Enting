# Financeable Consulting Live Site Sitemap

## Public launch pages

1. Home
   - Purpose: brand positioning, service overview, trust and conversion
   - URL: `/`
   - Primary CTA: `Book a consultation`

2. Monthly CFO
   - Purpose: explain recurring senior finance support
   - URL: `/services/monthly-cfo/`
   - Primary CTA: `Talk about monthly CFO support`

3. Financial Reporting
   - Purpose: explain management reporting, monthly visibility, and reporting discipline
   - URL: `/services/financial-reporting/`
   - Primary CTA: `Review your reporting setup`

4. Cashflow Management
   - Purpose: explain cash forecasting, planning rhythm, and finance support during growth pressure
   - URL: `/services/cashflow-management/`
   - Primary CTA: `Review your cash visibility`

5. About
   - Purpose: explain working style, fit, and advisory approach without inventing team biography details
   - URL: `/about/`
   - Primary CTA: `See how Financeable Consulting works`

6. Contact
   - Purpose: give a direct enquiry route and set expectations for the first conversation
   - URL: `/contact/`
   - Primary CTA: `Start an enquiry`

## Internal linking rules

- Home links to all three service pages, About, and Contact
- Each service page links laterally to the other two service pages
- Each service page links to Contact
- About links to Contact and the three services
- Contact links back to the service pages and Home

## Notes

- The live site is the publishable brand layer
- The 450-page matrix stays in the workspace as the later SEO expansion system
- Public launch pages should avoid fabricated credentials, reviews, phone numbers, and staff details
