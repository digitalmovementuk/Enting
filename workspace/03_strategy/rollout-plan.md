# Financeable Consulting Rollout Plan

## Launch site

Publish the core six-page website first.

- Total pages: 6
- Scope: homepage, three service pages, about, contact
- Goal: create a credible, conversion-focused brand site that can support paid traffic, referrals, and later SEO expansion
- Format: each page follows the shared content standard, uses researched service language, and includes clear enquiry paths

## Phase 1

Launch all `P1` pages first.

- Total pages: 45
- Scope: London
- Goal: establish high-intent commercial coverage for the most valuable keyword set after the brand site is live
- Format: every page uses the standard 1,500-word template with hero form and end-of-page Q&A

## Phase 2

Launch all `P2` pages next.

- Total pages: 84
- Scope: Manchester plus stronger inner-London districts
- Goal: expand proven page types into the next most relevant local markets
- Format: reuse the same master page structure to preserve consistency and production speed

## Phase 3

Launch all `P3` pages last.

- Total pages: 321
- Scope: remaining district and lower-priority page combinations
- Goal: broaden topical and geographic coverage after core pages are live
- Format: keep the same template, but allow lighter proof depth where commercial intent is weaker

## Service weighting

- Monthly CFO has the largest cluster and should lead the production effort.
- Financial Reporting should follow as the next strongest commercial service family.
- Cashflow Management should be prioritised around deliverables and startup-facing demand.
- Generic finance support pages should be used selectively to capture broader intent without diluting service clarity.

## Production requirement

No page should move into drafting without:

- the standard page template
- a defined CTA
- a hero-form variant
- at least 5 end-of-page Q&A entries
