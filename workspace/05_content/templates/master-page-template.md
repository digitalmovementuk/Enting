# Financeable Consulting Master Page Template

Use this structure for every service, deliverable, audience, and location page unless there is a strong reason to vary it.

## Page requirements

- Language: English
- Target length: `1,500` words
- Search target: one primary keyword and one closely related secondary theme
- Conversion requirement: hero contact form
- End-of-page requirement: Q&A section
- Optimization requirement: suitable for classic SEO and AI-search retrieval
- Quality requirement: AI-assisted drafts must be edited into specific, useful, people-first content before they are considered production-ready

## Structure

### 1. Hero section: `180-220 words`

Required elements:

- H1 with the exact primary keyword
- short supporting subheadline focused on commercial outcome
- 2-3 sentence credibility-led opening paragraph
- primary CTA button
- contact form embedded in the hero

Hero form fields:

- Name
- Work email
- Company
- Brief message

Suggested form heading:

- Speak to Financeable Consulting about {{PRIMARY_KEYWORD}}

### 2. What this service covers: `180-220 words`

Goal:

- define the service clearly in plain English
- explain what the client is buying
- introduce the context in which the service matters

### 3. Problems this solves: `180-220 words`

Goal:

- name the operational and financial pain points
- connect the problem to the audience's day-to-day reality
- make the commercial stakes explicit

### 4. How Financeable Consulting works: `220-260 words`

Goal:

- explain process, reporting cadence, delivery model, and communication style
- show how the service fits into a business without sounding generic

### 5. Deliverables and outcomes: `180-220 words`

Goal:

- list the outputs
- connect outputs to business value
- give the page enough specificity to rank for practical intent

### 6. Why choose Financeable Consulting: `180-220 words`

Goal:

- establish trust
- explain the difference between Financeable Consulting and a generic accountant, freelancer, or agency alternative

### 7. Call to action section: `100-140 words`

Goal:

- restate the fit
- reduce friction
- direct the visitor into the same conversion action as the hero

### 8. Q&A section: `220-280 words`

Requirement:

- minimum 5 questions
- concise, factual answers
- write them so they can be reused in FAQ schema and AI-search contexts

Recommended Q&A topics:

- what the service includes
- who it is for
- how the engagement works
- what the reporting cadence looks like
- when to choose this over an internal hire

## SEO requirements

- Include the primary keyword in the URL, title, H1, intro, and at least one H2
- Keep meta title and meta description unique
- Use direct, literal phrasing in definition sections
- Add internal links to the parent cluster page and related service pages
- Do not rely on keyword repetition as a substitute for topic depth

## AI-search requirements

- Write at least one short definition paragraph that answers the query directly
- Use clear entities and role descriptions
- Avoid vague marketing filler in the Q&A section
- Make each answer self-contained enough to stand alone in an extracted snippet

## AI-assisted content rules

- Do not leave generic AI phrasing unedited
- Replace broad claims with concrete service detail wherever possible
- If proof, credentials, or examples are missing, mark that gap for review instead of inventing confidence
- Keep sibling pages meaningfully differentiated, especially across locations
