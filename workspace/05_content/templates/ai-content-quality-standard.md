# Financeable Consulting AI Content Quality Standard

## Rule

AI-assisted content is acceptable only when it is turned into useful, specific, people-first material.

## Use AI for

- first drafts
- structure
- section ordering
- FAQ drafting
- metadata drafting

## Do not stop at AI draft

Every page still needs:

- query-intent alignment
- commercial clarity
- factual review
- removal of generic phrasing
- differentiation from nearby pages in the same cluster

## Failure signs

- the page could fit almost any consultancy
- location pages differ only by swapped place names
- the copy repeats benefits without explaining delivery
- the Q&A adds no new information
- the page sounds confident without giving evidence

## Standard for Financeable Consulting

- direct definitions
- practical financial language
- clear scope of service
- realistic claims
- useful answers for both search visitors and AI retrieval systems
