# Page Production Checklist

- Primary keyword is defined before drafting
- Page follows the 1,500-word Financeable Consulting master template
- Hero includes an embedded contact form
- Q&A section includes at least 5 questions
- Copy is written in English
- The page has one clear CTA
- Intro defines the service directly
- Metadata is unique
- Internal links are mapped
- The page reads cleanly in both SEO and AI-search contexts
- Generic AI filler has been removed
- The page has enough specificity to stand apart from sibling pages
- Claims, examples, and proof points are either supported or clearly marked for review
