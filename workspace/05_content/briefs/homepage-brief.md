# Homepage Brief

## Primary intent

Convert businesses that need practical senior finance support into consultation enquiries.

## Core messages

- Financeable Consulting helps growing businesses create clearer monthly visibility and stronger financial decisions
- The offer sits between a basic accountant relationship and a full-time senior finance hire
- The three core services are Monthly CFO, Financial Reporting, and Cashflow Management

## Audience

- founders
- owner-managed businesses
- small leadership teams
- startups under finance pressure
- service businesses that have outgrown informal finance handling

## Homepage requirements

- lead with a clear value proposition and a hero contact form
- explain the three services quickly
- make the buying problems visible without resorting to fear language
- use a reassurance section instead of fake reviews
- include service-area context without inventing an office address

## CTA direction

- Book a consultation
- Talk through your finance setup
- Start with a practical finance review
