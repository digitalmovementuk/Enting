# Contact Brief

## Primary intent

Reduce friction and help a prospect understand what happens after they enquire.

## Core messages

- early conversations are practical and low-pressure
- Financeable Consulting can review current reporting, cash visibility, and monthly finance needs
- the contact route should feel simple and clear

## Contact-page requirements

- include a prominent form
- explain what a first conversation is for
- list the three core service areas
- include service-area context only where safely supported
- answer common contact objections in FAQ form

## CTA direction

- Start an enquiry
- Book a consultation
- Share your finance challenge
