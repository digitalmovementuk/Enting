# Monthly CFO Master Brief

## Primary intent

Convert business owners searching for ongoing senior finance support, fractional CFO capability, or monthly finance leadership.

## Core themes

- Monthly CFO
- Fractional CFO
- Virtual CFO
- Finance director support
- Part-time finance team
- Outsourced finance leadership

## Messaging angle

Financeable Consulting provides ongoing finance leadership without the cost and commitment of a full-time senior hire.

## Proof points to gather

- founder or consultant credentials
- reporting examples
- strategic finance process
- meeting cadence
- planning and forecasting support

## CTA direction

- Book a consultation
- Talk through your finance setup
- Request a monthly CFO scope

## Build standard

- Use the shared 1,500-word master page template
- Include the contact form in the hero section
- End with a Q&A block
- Make the page legible for both traditional SEO and AI-search extraction
