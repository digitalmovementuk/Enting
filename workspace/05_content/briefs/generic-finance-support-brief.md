# Generic Finance Support Brief

## Primary intent

Capture broad finance-support demand without replacing the clearer service clusters.

## Core themes

- Finance support
- Finance help
- Finance business partner
- Outsourced finance team

## Messaging angle

Use these pages to qualify broad demand and route visitors into the strongest service path.

## Guardrail

Keep generic pages commercially useful, but avoid letting them outrank or cannibalise the core Monthly CFO, Financial Reporting, and Cashflow Management pages.

## Build standard

- Use the shared 1,500-word master page template
- Include the contact form in the hero section
- End with a Q&A block
- Make the page legible for both traditional SEO and AI-search extraction
