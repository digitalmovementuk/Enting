# About Brief

## Primary intent

Build trust by explaining how Financeable Consulting works and what makes the service commercially useful.

## Core messages

- the business is focused on practical finance support, not generic advisory fluff
- clients get clearer reporting, stronger cash visibility, and more useful monthly decision support
- the delivery model is flexible and designed to fit growing businesses before a full-time hire is justified

## Constraints

- do not invent founder biography, staff names, qualifications, or tenure
- write around the advisory model and working style instead
- keep the page calm, credible, and useful

## CTA direction

- See if the working style fits your business
- Start a conversation about your finance setup
